The slopes of parallel lines are **identical**
The slopes of perpendicular lines are **negative reciprocal**

>[!NOTE] Example
>$$-8x+8y=0 \\ x - y = 4$$
>$\mathtt{[1]} 8y=-8x \\ \mathtt{[2]} -y = -x + 4$
>$\mathtt{[1]} y=x \\ \mathtt{[2]} y=x-4 \\ parallel$

>[!NOTE] Example
>$$6x+4y=-4 \\ y-1=\frac{2}{3}(x+9)$$
>$\mathtt{[1]} 4y=-6x-4 \\ \mathtt{[2]} y-1=\frac{2}{3}x + 6$
>$y=-\frac{3}{2}x-1 \\ y=\frac{2}{3}x+7$
>$perpendicular$
