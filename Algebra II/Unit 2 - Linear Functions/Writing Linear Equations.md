## Point-slope Formula
$$y-y_1 = m(x-x_1)$$
>[!NOTE] Examples
>**If (-1,7) and m= -4**
>$$y-7=-4(x+1) \\ y-7=-4x-4 \\ y=-4x+3$$
## Examples of Finding Slope-Intercept Form with Two Points
**If (-6, 1) and (-3, 7)**
$$\mathtt{find \space slope} \\ 
\frac{7-1}{-3+6}=\frac{6}{3}=2 \\ 
\mathtt{plug \space into \space formula} \\ 
y-1=2(x+6) \\ y-1=2x+12 \\ y=2x+13$$
