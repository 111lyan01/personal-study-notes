## Slope Formulas
**Slope Formula**
Slope is in the form of a fraction, of y over x, or rise over run.
$$m=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}$$
**Special slopes**
$m = \frac{0}{x}$ is a horizontal line. The slope is 0.
$m=\frac{x}{0}$ is a vertical line. The slope is undefined.

**Slope Intercept Form**
$$y=mx+b$$
**Representing horizontal and vertical lines**
$$horizontal: \space y= num$$
$$vertical: \space x = num$$
**Standard Form**
$$ax+by=c$$
## Converting Forms Example
$$5x-6y=12$$
$$5x(-5x)-6y=(-5x)12 \Rightarrow -6y=-5x+12$$
$$\frac{-6y=-5x+12}{-6} \Rightarrow y=\frac{5}{6}x-2$$
## Find X and Y Intercepts Example
**Equation**
$$y=-5x-4$$
**Y Intercept**
$$y=-5(0)-4 \Rightarrow y=-4$$
**X Intercept**$$y(0)=-5x-4 \Rightarrow 0=-5x-4$$
$$0=-5x(+5x)-4 \Rightarrow 5x=-4$$
$$\frac{5x=-4}{5} \Rightarrow x=-\frac{4}{5}$$
