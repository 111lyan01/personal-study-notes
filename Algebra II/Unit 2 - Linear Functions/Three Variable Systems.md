1. Choose two equations
	1. eliminate one variable
2. Choose two equations, where one equation is the same from the previous step and one equation is new (the only unused equation)
	1. eliminate the same variable you removed in the first step
3. Put the equations you got from the first and second step together and solve
4. Substitute your answers from the third equation into an original to find the last variable
## Example
$$4x+y-3z=45 \\ 6x-4y+3z = 41 \\ 2x-7y-11z=85 \\
\mathtt{-------------------} \\
4x+y-3z=45 \\ +(6x-4y+3z=41) \\
\Rightarrow 10x-3y=86 \\ 
\mathtt{-------------------} \\
(6x-4y+3z=41)11 \\ (2x-7y-11z = 85)3 \\
\Rightarrow 66x-44y+33z=451 \\ +(6x-21y-33z = 255) \\
72x-65y = 706 \\
\mathtt{-------------------} \\ 
(72x-65y=706)3 \\ (10x-3y=86)65 \\
\Rightarrow 216x-195y = 2118 \\ -(650x-195y = 5590) \\
-434x=-3472 \Rightarrow x=8 \\
\mathtt{-------------------} \\ 
650(8)-195y=5590 \\
5200 - 195y = 5590 \\
-195y=390 \\
y=-2 \\
\mathtt{-------------------} \\ 
4(8)-2-3z=45 \\
32-2-3z=45 \\
30-3z=45 \\
-3z = 15 \\
z = -5 \\
\mathtt{-------------------} \\ 
\{8, -2, -5\}
$$
