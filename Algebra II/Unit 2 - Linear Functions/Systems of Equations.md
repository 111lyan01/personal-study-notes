## Solving with Substitution
1. Isolate the variable
2. Substitute the equation into the other equation
3. Solve
4. Substitute your solved equation to find the other variable

>[!NOTE] Example
>$$2y=6x+10 \\ 3x-y=5$$
>$y=3x+5$
>$3x-(3x+5)=5$
>$3x-3x-5=5$
>$-5=5$
>$false/no \space solution$
## Solving with Elimation
1. Line up equations
2. Multiply the equations so match a variable
3. Eliminate the variable
4. Solve
5. Substitute

>[!NOTE] Example
>$$7x-6y=-53 \\ 2x-3y=-13$$
>$2x-3y=-13 \rightarrow 4x-6y=-26$
>$3x=-27$
>$x=-9$
>$y=-\frac{5}{3}$
