1. Isolate the absolute value expression
2. Create two cases, one to equal a positive and the other negative
3. Solve both solutions
4. Check for extraneous solutions

>[!NOTE] Extraneous Solutions: Solutions that do not work in their original equation
>Example:
>$$|8-2n|-5n=n+2$$
>$8-2n=-6n-2$ and $8-2n=6n+2$
>$8=-4n-2$ and $8=8n+2$
>$10=-4n$ and $6=8n$
>$n = -\frac{5}{2}$ and $n=\frac{3}{4}$
>CHECK: $8−2(-\frac{5}{2})−5(-\frac{5}{2}) = 25.5$, $-\frac{5}{2}+2 = -0.5$
>$\mathtt{answers \space don't \space match}$
