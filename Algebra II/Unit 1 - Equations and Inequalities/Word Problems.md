>[!NOTE] Examples
>"Kara's golf score was 1 less than three fourth's of Greg's gold score. If the sum of their scores was 153, find Kara's gold score." Can be converted to
>$$k=\frac{3}{4}-1$$
>$$k+g=153$$
>$\frac{3}{4}g-1+g=153$
>$\frac{7}{4}g-1=153$
>$\frac{7}{4}g=154$
>$g=\frac{616}{7}$
>$g=88$
>$k+88=153$
>$k=65$
>
>"The measure of one angle is six more than half the measure of another angle. If the angles are complementary, find both measures" Can be converted to
>$$x+\frac{1}{2}x+6=90$$
>$\frac{3}{2}x+6=90$
>$\frac{3}{2}x=84$
>$x=56$
>$\mathtt{angle \space 1=56, \space angle \space 2=34}$
>
>"Find three consecutive even integers such that the sum of the smallest integer and twice the median integer is 20 more than the largest integer" In this question, it's trying to find three consecutive even integers, so it's trying to find this order:
>$x-2$
>$x$
>$x+2$
>So it wants the sum of the smallest integer ($x-2$), plus the median ($x$) to be 20 more the than largest integer ($x+2$). This can be expressed as:
>$$x-2+2x=x+2+20$$
>$3x-2=x+22$
>$3x=x+24$
>$2x=24$
>$x = 12$
