## Simplifying Radicals
1. Find the largest perfect square of the number
2. Square root the perfect square, taking out the radical
3. Leave the rest under the radical.

>[!NOTE] Examples
>$\sqrt{288}$
>$= \sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{144}$
>$\sqrt{144} \rightarrow 12$
>$12 \sqrt{2}$
