1. Distribute
2. Simplify sides
3. Move variables
4. Solve

>[!NOTE] Example
>$$\frac{10x+17-(x-9)}{2}=3x+1$$
>$= (10x+17-(x-9)) \div 2=3x+1$ 
>$= (10x+17-x+9) \div 2=3x+1$ 
>$= (10x+26-x) \div 2=3x+1$
>$= (9x+26) \div 2=3x+1$
>$= (9x+26)=2(3x+1)$
>$= 9x+26=6x+2$
>$= 3x+26=2$
>$= 3x=-24$
>$= x=-8$

>[!WARNING] Special Cases
>There are two special cases where there could be no solutions or infinite solutions. For example:
>$$1+3c=5(1-c)+8c$$
>$1+3c=5-5c+8c$
>$1+3c=5+3c$
>$3c=3c+4$
>$\mathtt{no \space solutions}$
>$$40-8x=-8(x-5)$$
>$40-8x=-8x+40$
>$40=40$
>$\mathtt{infinite \space solutions}$

>[!NOTE] Proportions
>Cross multiply.
>$$\frac{10}{5x-9}=\frac{8}{2x-8}$$
>$10(2x-8)=8(5x-9)$
>$20x-80=40x-72$
>$20x=40x+8$
>$-20x=8$
>$x=-\frac{2}{5}$
