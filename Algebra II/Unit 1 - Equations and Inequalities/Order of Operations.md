## Evaluating Expressions
Expressions that have variables defined simply have their variables replaced.

>[!NOTE] Example
>If $a=3, \space b=-4, \space c=8$:
>$$\frac{a \times b^2}{c+12}$$
>$= (3 \times (-4)^2) \div (8+12)$
>$= (3 \times 16) \div (20)$
>$= 48 \div 20$
>$= \frac{12}{5}$
## Combining Like-Terms
Simplify expressions by distributing or adding/subtracting like terms

>[!NOTE] Example
>$$13+2(3n-7)+19n$$
>$= 13+6n-14+19n$
>$= 13-14+25n$
>$= -1+25n$
