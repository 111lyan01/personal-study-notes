* The Consitution is a product of the colonists' experiences with government
* Grounded by political philosphies from the 1700s, including John Locke and John Hobbes
# The Colonies
* First New England colony was established in 1620
* Religious separatists came on the *Mayflower* and drew the *Mayflower Compact* 
	* Compact agreed to create and submit to authoirty of government
	* Depends on the consent of the governed
* By 1732 all 13 colonies were established
* Colonies were governed by London but they could make their own laws too
* British government began to enacts laws that taxed the colonies to recover war costs
* Boston Tea Party over the Tea Act, led to closing of Boston Harbor from Intolerable Acts
# Colonial Response
* Colonies got together and held colonial congressational meetings
* First Congress led to a petition to the king (didn't work)
* Second Congress led to talks about an Army
* Thomas Paine's *Common Sense* argued for independence
