## Types of Government
### Totalitarianism
Government that controls all aspects of political and social life. **North Korea** is one such example of a totalitarianism regime.
* Citizens do not have the freedom of speech nor to assemble and seek solutions
### Authoritarianism
High concentrated and centralized power maintained by political repression. **China** is one such example of this.
* Only the government is entirely controlled by one ruler, and social and economic aspects can be partially controlled by external forces
* One party rules with no competition
* The general public has no input on matters.
### Aristocracy
In theory, rule by the most qualified, but in practice only the upper class rules. An **oligarchy** is rule by a small group of corrupt and self-serving officials.
### Democracy
Government that is controlled by the people. 
### Anarchy
The lack of government.
## Direct Democracy
A democracy where all people discussed and voted directly on all laws. Some democracies today have aspects of direct democracy, such as an **initiative** or **referendum**, where people can vote directly on laws or amendments. The **recall** process allows people to vote others out of office.
### The Limits of Direct Democracy
When the government of the United States was being drafted, James Madison warned the concerns of a "pure democracy", in which a majority mob rule could undercut the representation of minority groups.
## Democratic Republic
The United States Constitution created a democratic republic, where people hold the power through an electron process, but elected officials decide on matters of the government. Initially, the Senate and President were selected by elites, but amendments gave this power of the people.
### Principals of Democracy
Initially in the United States, only white males were able to vote. Only gradually through activism and avocation did universal suffrage become the rule. Democracies also add protections to guarantee the rights of political or ethnic minorities.

The freedom of speech is also protected so that people can speak out against problems of the government. 

Another concept introduced is limited government. In the United States, an intricate system of checks and balances are put in place to make sure government doesn't have too much power.
## Majoritarianism
When the government does what the majority of the people want. This is not an accurate representation of most democracies however, because sometimes what the majority wants is at the cost of minority groups. For example, the majority group may vote against same-sex marriage or the banning of slavery, where other people like the president, Congress, or the Supreme Court can change these decisions. 
## Pluralism
Pluralist theory says that if someone cannot keep up with politics, their interests will be represented by a group. Pluralism defines politics as a struggle between groups that want their way for their members. Pluralism is often described in the United States. But it has some fatal issues. Poor citizens are often underrepresented by groups while rich citizens are overrepresented, and group decision-making doesn't always represent the interests of the public.
