Generally used for actions done or repeated in the present.
>[!NOTE] Examples
>```
>I get good grades => Yo saco buenos notas
>Ana writes with her left hand => Ana escribe con su mano izquierda
>```
# Types
## Regular
1. Identify verb ending (ar, er, ir)
2. Remove verb ending
3. Add appropriate ending to stem

| form              | ar   | er   | ir   |
| ----------------- | ---- | ---- | ---- |
| yo                | o    | o    | o    |
| tu                | as   | es   | es   |
| el/ella/usted     | a    | e    | e    |
| nosotros          | amos | emos | imos |
| vosotros          | áis  | éis  | ís   |
| ello/ella/ustedes | an   | en   | en   |

>[!NOTE] Examples
>```
>hablar               comer
>hablo    hablamos    como    comemos
>hablas   habláis     comes   coméis
>habla    hablan      come    comen
>```

## Stem-Changers
Verbs that change their endings in all forms but nosotros and vosotros form. Modify the last vowel (excluding the verb ending) and apply the following

| Vowel | Stem-Changed |
| ----- | ------------ |
| o     | ue           |
| e     | ie           |
| e     | i            |

>[!NOTE] Examples
>```
>pedir =>
>pido     pedimos
>pides    pedís
>pide     piden
>```
