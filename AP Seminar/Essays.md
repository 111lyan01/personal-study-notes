## Three-Read Strategy
### #1 - Around the Text
* Title
* Subheadings
* Pictures and captions
* Graphs and Charts
### #2 - Intro/Conclusion
* Subheadings and signals
* A paragraph on each end
* Keep the title in mind
### #3 - ACE
* **A**rgument
* **C**laims
* **E**vidance
