A word that stands for a noun or another person. The words that pronouns stand for are antecedents. There are several pronoun types.

| Type       | Definition                                                                                          | Example                                               |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Personal   | Refers to the person speaking, spoken to, or person, place, thing, or idea being spoken about       | I, me, we, us, you, it, he, him, she, her, they, them |
| Possessive | Indicating ownership or possessive                                                                  | my, mine, our, ours, your, its, his, hers             |
| Reflexive  | End in -self or -selves to indicate that someone or something in the sentence acts for or on itself | myself, ourselves, yourself, herself                  |
| Intensive  | End in -self or -selves to add emphasis to a noun or pronoun in the sentence                        | yourselves, itself, himself, themselves               |
| Reciprocal | Refer to a plural antecedent and express a mutual action or relationship                            | each other, one another                               |
