Adverbs modify a ver, adjective, or another adverb. They typically answer the following questions:

|                     |                                              |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Where?**          | The book was ~={blue}here=~                  |
| **When?**           | He ~={blue}never=~ walked the dog            |
| **In what way?**    | Thomas ~={blue}gently=~ corrected her        |
| **To what extent?** | They ~={blue}completely=~ lost track of time |

>[!NOTE] Example
>**Thomas always runs really fast.**
>There are two adverbs in this sentence.
>**always** is affecting **runs** and **really** is affecting **fast**.
