Adverbs modify a ver, adjective, or another adverb. They typically answer the following questions:

|                     |                                                                                  |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where?**          | The book was <span style="color:rgb(137, 180, 250)">here</span>                  |
| **When?**           | He <span style="color:rgb(137, 180, 250)">never</span> walked the dog            |
| **In what way?**    | Thomas <span style="color:rgb(137, 180, 250)">gently</span> corrected her        |
| **To what extent?** | They <span style="color:rgb(137, 180, 250)">completely</span> lost track of time |

>[!NOTE] Example
>**Thomas always runs really fast.**
>There are two adverbs in this sentence.
>**always** is affecting **runs** and **really** is affecting **fast**.
