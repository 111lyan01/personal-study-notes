A preposition relates the noun or pronoun that appears with it to another word in the sentence. A prepositional phrase is a group of words that includes a preposition and a noun or pronoun, as well as any modifiers.

>[!NOTE] Examples
>The computer is **~={red}on=~ the ~={green}table=~**
>We need to go the school **~={red}at=~ ~={green}3 o'clock=~**
>She lives **~={red}in=~ the ~={green}United Kingdom=~**
>I drove to school **~={red}without=~ my ~={green}backpack=~**
