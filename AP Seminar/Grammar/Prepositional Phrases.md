A preposition relates the noun or pronoun that appears with it to another word in the sentence. A prepositional phrase is a group of words that includes a preposition and a noun or pronoun, as well as any modifiers.

>[!NOTE] Examples

>We need to go the school **<span style="color:rgb(166, 227, 161)">at</span> <span style="color:rgb(243, 139, 168)">3 o'clock</span>**
>She lives **<span style="color:rgb(166, 227, 161)">in</span> the <span style="color:rgb(243, 139, 168)">United Kingdom</span>**
>I drove to school **<span style="color:rgb(166, 227, 161)">without</span> my <span style="color:rgb(243, 139, 168)">backpack</span>**
