Verbs are words that describe an action, condition, occurrence, or state of being. There are two types.

| Type    | Defintion                                                                | Example                                                                                   |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Action  | Tells what action someone/something is performing                        | go: goes, is going. went, has gone<br>run: runs, is running, ran, has fun                 |
| Linking | Connects a subject with a noun, pronoun, of adjective that describes it. | be: am, is, are, is being, was, were, has been<br>feel: feels, is feeling, felt, has felt |
