Words that connect words, phrases, or clauses.
## Types

|                   |                                                                                                        |                                                                                                |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Coordinating**  | Seven exist. They connect similar pars of speech or groups of words that have equal grammatical weight | For an nor but or yet so                                                                       |
| **Correlative**   | Five exist and are paired. They join elements of equal grammatical weight                              | both/and ... either/or ... neither/nor ... not only/but also ... whether/or                    |
| **Subordinating** | Join two complete ideas by making one of the ideas dependent on the other                              | after / because / although / whenever / when / where / as though / in order that / as long as  |
>[!NOTE] Examples
>I know the computer is fun, **but** you need to go outside
>She talks to her friend **after** she plays games.
>**Either** you keep reading The Book Thief, **or** you keep studying.
