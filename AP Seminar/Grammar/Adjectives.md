Adjectives are used to describe a noun or pronoun or to give it a more specific meaning. They usually answer one of **four questions**: 
```
gardens
	what kind of gardens?
		Flower gardens
lesson
	which lesson?
		that lesson
years
	how many years
		sixty-eight years
energy
	how much energy?
		boundless energy
```

>[!NOTE] Example
>**Ana's computer is really fast**
>The adjective **fast** is affecting **computer**.
>The [[Adverbs|adverb]] **really** is affecting **fast**.
