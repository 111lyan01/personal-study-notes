## Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle
"One cannot simultaneously determine both the position and momentum of an electron"

| You can find...  | But not...      |
| ---------------- | --------------- |
| Where it is      | Where its going |
| Where it's going | Where it is     |
## Electron Energy Levels
Each possible distance from the nucleus
<span style="color:rgb(137, 180, 250)">n=electron shell</span>
Electrons that can fit in a shell: $2n^2$
## Electron Orbital
An orbital region within an energy level where there is a chance of finding an electron
<span style="color:rgb(243, 139, 168)">Only 2 electrons can be contained in an orbital!</span>
