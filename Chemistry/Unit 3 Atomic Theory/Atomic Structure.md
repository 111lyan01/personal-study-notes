## John Dalton
English scientist how developed atomic theory by experiments based on the law of constant composition
## Law of Constant Composition
A given compound always contains elements in exactly the same proportion by mass
## Dalton's Atomic Theory
1. Elements are made of tiny particles called atoms
2. All atoms of an element are identical
3. The atoms of an element are different from those of other elements
4. Atoms of an element can combine with other elements to form compounds. A compound always has the same relative number and types of atoms
5. Atoms of indivisible in chemical processes (~={red}modern atomic theory says that nuclear reactions CAN split, create, or destroy atoms=~)
## Discovery of the Electron
J.J Thomson used a cathode ray rube to deduce the presence of a negatively charged particle. Cathode ray tubes pass electricity through a gas that is contained at a very low pressure.
### Conclusions from the Study
1. All elements must contain identically charged electrons
2. Atoms are neutral. Position particles must exist that balance the negative charge of electrons
3. Electrons have very little mass, such that atoms must have another particle to account for its mass
## Thomson's Atomic Model
Electrons are like plums embedded in a positively charged pudding
## Rutherford's Gold Foil Experiment
Alpha particles (helium nuclei) were fired at a thin sheet of gold foil. The particle hits a detecting screen wrapped around the gold foil and records their place.
### Findings
Most particles passed through
Some were deflected
Very few were greatly deflected

**The nucleus is small.**
**The nucleus is dense.**
**The nucleus is positively charged**

## Isotopes
Atoms of an element having varying masses due to neutron count
