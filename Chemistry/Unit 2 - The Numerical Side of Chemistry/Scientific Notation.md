When you see a number like $$\texttt{2.38e20}$$
in your calculator, what does the "e" mean? Well, the "e" simply means "times 10 to the power of", so this number is the exact same as $$2.38\times10^{20}$$
To input exponentials on your calculator, use the `EE` button to insert an exponential to the current number inputted.
