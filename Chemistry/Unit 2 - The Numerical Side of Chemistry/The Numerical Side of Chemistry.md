### When is a digit uncertain?
When there is an amount that is estimated

>[!NOTE] Example
>There are "around 2000" kids at John Adams.
### Accuracy
The agreement of a particular value with a true value
### Precision
The degreee of agreement among several measurements made in the same manner
## Significant Figures
A way to signify the digits that are significant/accurate in an estimated number
>[!INFO]
>See [[Rules for Significant Figures]] to learn more.
### Error Types
**Random Errors:** (Indeterminate error)
The measurement has an equal probability of being high or low
**Systematic Errors:** (Determinate Error)
Occurs in the same direction each time (high or low), often resulting from poor techniquie or incorrect calibration
