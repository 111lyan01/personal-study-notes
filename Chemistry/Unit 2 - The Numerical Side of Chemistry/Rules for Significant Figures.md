**Nonzero integers** always count as significant figures
>[!NOTE] Example
>**3456** has **4** significant figures
## Zeros
* **Leading zeros** do not count as significant figures
* **Zeros inbetween numbers** always count as significant figures
* **Trailing zeros** are significant **only** if it's after a decimal point

>[!NOTE] Examples
>```
>Number                     Count
>1.0070                     5
>17.10                      4
>3.29 * 10^3                3
>0.0054                     2
>3,200,000                  2
>```

## Mathematical Operations
* **Multiplication and Division:** Number of digits in the least precise measurement equal number of sig figs
>[!NOTE] Examples
>```
>Number                     Count
>3.24 * 7.0                 2
>100.0 / 23.7               3
>0.02 * 2.371               1
>710 / 3.0                  2
>1818.2 * 3.23              3
>1.030 / 2.87               3
>```
