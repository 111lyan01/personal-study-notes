Using a series of conversions, you can convert units without needing the absolute knowledge of all conversions. For example, how many liters would it take to equal 1 gallon? If we know that: `1 gal => 4 quarts and 1.057 quarts => 1 L`, we can use this to figure out our answer: $$\frac{1 \space gal}{1} \times \frac{4 \space qts}{1 \space gal} \times \frac{1 \space L}{1.057 qts} = \frac{4}{1.057} \Rightarrow 3.784 \space L$$
