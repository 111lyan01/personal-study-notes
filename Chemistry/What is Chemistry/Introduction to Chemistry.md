## Terms
### Science
Experimental Investigations and explainations of naturally occuring events
### Technology
The application of scientific technology
### Chemistry
The study of matter and its transformations
### Matter
Anything with mass and form
### Transformation
Changes in matter
### Pure Substance
A single type of matter
### Mixture
Two or more subtances intermingled
## Mixtures, Substances, and Elements
### Homogenous Mixture
Mixtures with a uniform composition
### Heterogenous Mixture
Mixtures with a varied composition with mixed ratios across substances
### Elements
The building blocks of all matter, 118 (as of now) discovered on the periodic table
### Elemental Symbols
Represented with one or two letters.
### Atoms
The smallest structural units of elements
### Pure Substances Types
**Elements:** Only one element
**Compounds:** 2+ elements
### Compounds
A pure substance of 2+ elements (also a molecule)
